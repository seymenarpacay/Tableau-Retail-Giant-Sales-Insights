# Tableau-Retail-Giant-Sales-Insights
This study analyzes the sales profitability and on-time delivery success of the Sample Superstore dataset, which contains sales data from a large logistics company and is used as an example dataset in Tableu.

As seen in this dataset, each row represents product records ordered by the customer and completed the shipping process. The columns represent the characteristics of these records, such as location (country, city, state, postal code, region), product category, order and delivery dates, and product category.

A) Sales Profitability Analysis

1- Time Series for Sales and Profit: I visualized total sales and profit by month. It can be seen that sales and profit generally follow a proportional pattern. In this respect, the company has a stable structure. The fact that profit sometimes appears larger than sales in the visual may be confusing; this is due to the fact that the same graph has two different y-axes.

2- Profitability Analysis for Product Categories: This was created to perform sales profitability analysis based on delivery type, product category, and subcategory information. A color scale ranging from green to red was used to identify combinations that provide the highest profit/sales ratio. In this regard, the Labels, Paper, and Envelopes products in the Office Supplies category stand out with their success, while the Tables product in the Furniture category stands out with its failure. It is noteworthy that the success of the Supplies product varies considerably depending on the delivery type.

B) Shipping Speed ​​Analysis

1- Shipping Speed ​​On Ship Mode: This section visualizes the shipping time depending on the delivery type. First Class shipping is honestly the most successful delivery type, as Same Day is by nature the fastest.

2- Order Delivery Speed: This section visualizes the number of records for each day from order to shipment. It is understood that delivery generally takes place within 4 days of the order, and the distribution shows a tendency in this direction.

3- Average Shipment Duration For Locations: Average delivery times are visualized according to state location. There is no geographical trend towards any particular region, which shows that the company conducts its operations entirely according to customer requests when making deliveries and that the logistics process is not affected by distance. The North Dakota region stands out with an average delivery time of 2.9 days.
